Why ChargeNear?

Connector match

Pick your vehicle type and we only show chargers your connector fits: CCS2, CHAdeMO, Type 2 or Bharat AC-001.

Slot booking

Reserve a time slot in advance with simple, validated forms so you never queue at a busy station.

Cost estimate

Industry context

Electric mobility is one of the fastest growing parts of India's clean energy sector. Public charging infrastructure is still sparse, and drivers often cannot tell which station is free, fast or compatible. ChargeNear is a web-based charging network directory and booking front end aimed at that gap.
